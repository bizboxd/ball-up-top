Every pickup run has someone who cooked. Now you can prove it.

Who Cooked gives your pickup games player ratings like the pros get. Log your soccer, basketball and volleyball runs, set your formation, and rate your teammates out of 10 after the final whistle.

HOW IT WORKS
• Start a game and invite your squad. Uneven teams? 7v8 is fine.
• Two captains, two formations, one score.
• After the game, rate every teammate and the ref from 0.1 to 10.
• Ratings stay hidden until voting closes, then everyone finds out at once.
• Man of the match goes to the top-rated player.

MORE THAN A SCORE
• Profiles with your overall rating, form, record, badges and every game you've played
• Add your own stats: goals, assists, points, kills and more
• Leaderboards by month and all time, plus a table for the refs
• Banter under every game, with replies
• Match cards you can share to your story or the group chat
• Friends, RSVPs and an inbox so nobody misses a run

FAIR BY DESIGN
• You only rate your own teammates, never yourself
• A rating only counts once at least 2 people have rated you
• Report or block anyone, any time

Who Cooked is free. Find your squad and find out who cooked.
