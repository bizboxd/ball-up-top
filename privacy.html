Privacy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Who Cooked is an app for logging pickup games and rating your teammates. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address or phone number, or the name and email Apple or Google share when you sign in with them.
  • Profile: your username, display name and profile picture, if you add one.
  • What you do in the app: games you create or join, teams, formations, scores, your own stats, the ratings you give, RSVPs, comments and friend connections.
  • Purchases: which plan you're on and when it renews. Apple or Stripe handle the payment itself; we never see your card details.

Who can see it

Other signed-in players can see your profile, games, stats and comments. Ratings stay hidden until a game's voting window closes, and then show as averages. We don't show who gave which rating.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the app's data and handles sign-in.
  • Free players may see ads from Google (AdMob in the iPhone app, AdSense on the website). We ask for non-personalized ads and don't share your profile, ratings or games with advertisers. Pro removes ads.
  • RevenueCat tracks whether you have Pro. Apple processes purchases in the iPhone app; Stripe processes purchases on the website.
  • Apple and Google, only if you choose to sign in with them.

We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from your profile in the app. That permanently removes your profile, ratings, comments and stats. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription, so cancel it first in your App Store settings or from your profile.

Children

Who Cooked isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
