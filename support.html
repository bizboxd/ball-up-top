Support

Need help with Who Cooked? Here's how to reach us.

Contact

Email info.sportsboxd@gmail.com. We reply within 2 business days.

Report a comment or player

Tap the flag next to any comment to report it, or open a player's profile and tap Report. You can also Block someone from their profile so you no longer see their comments. We review reports within 24 hours.

Forgot your password

On the login screen, tap Forgot password? and we'll email you a link to pick a new one.

Delete your account

Open your profile (Me), scroll down and tap Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, ratings, comments and stats.

Who Cooked Pro

Pro is optional. To cancel a subscription bought in the iPhone app, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore a purchase, use Restore purchases on the Pro page.
